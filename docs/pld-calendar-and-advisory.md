# PLD calendar and monthly advisory

The chart binds its grain and date domain to the last completed response. Pending or failed queries cannot reinterpret cached monthly values as daily/hourly values. Calendar buckets retain missing dates, with no zero fill or line across a gap. Monthly labels cover the queried months, daily labels include weekday/date, and hourly labels include date/time in America/Sao_Paulo. Daily and hourly windows can be navigated without new requests. Hourly filters are visibly adjusted to the supported inclusive 32-day range before submission.

In the backoffice PLD chart only, a persistent summary and both pointer/keyboard and native point tooltips show each selected submarket's period mean. It is the sum of price times observed hours divided by observed hours over the complete returned calendar query, never the paginated table or the graph's smaller viewport. Partial coverage uses its actual observed hours; gaps and markets without observations do not become zero. Monthly queries use the displayed monthly references and disclose that basis. Applying another calendar range recalculates the mean; navigating the graph window does not change it. The client Portal price dashboard is unaffected.

Monthly advice reads at most 24 closed calendar months up to the query end, capped at the current Sao Paulo date. An official monthly reference is preferred over an aggregation of complete hourly observations. Required hours are the actual calendar month days times 24. Partial months never enter the monthly assessment, and no daily/hourly records are synthesized from a monthly average.

CCEE_MONTHLY_ADVISORY_1 compares the latest consecutive months (up to three) with up to 12 actual monthly references. P25 and P75 use linear interpolation and require six complete monthly references. The interface shows which observed months fall in each band. Sensitivities use recent mean times (1 + percentage / 100), with -15/-10/-5/0/+5/+10/+15 percent. These are conditional scenarios, not dated buying instructions or a statistical forecast. Existing daily backtest/coverage release rules remain intact. A year-equivalent comparison requires 24 consecutive actual months.

Automatic documentary association is a separate authenticated GET to the existing guidance route. It uses bounded local Portuguese text search over currently eligible published releases. It never calls embeddings, Search, an LLM or CCEE SOAP. Role, organization, license and publication gates are delegated to the existing guarded publication functions; page content and hashes are rehydrated and revalidated before display. The operation records zero external requests/tokens/cost in the existing retrieval audit. The explicit POST guidance remains the separately budgeted expanded retrieval. No retrieved answers or normative contents are cached.

Apply the compatible migration 20261228_ccee_market_assessment.sql before deploying the consumer. It adds two server-only functions and no tables. No credential, budget, continuous collection configuration, contract, settlement or access grant changes. Existing monthly/daily source reads remain available and identified separately from advisory and documentary results.

### Visão automática pelo calendário

No PLD do backoffice, a visão automática usa horas para um único dia, dias para intervalos de dois dias até dois meses de calendário e meses para períodos maiores. Alterar as datas reativa a escolha automática; a seleção manual continua disponível. A consulta usa apenas observações reais da granularidade escolhida; referências mensais não são convertidas em dados diários ou horários. O gráfico preserva a última consulta concluída enquanto carrega uma nova.

A leitura do ponto e a média do período ficam em uma tabela abaixo do gráfico, sem sobreposição. A seleção permanece ao retirar o mouse, e as descrições acessíveis dos pontos substituem os balões nativos do SVG.
