EVA closing: demand-excess classification and nominal monthly costs

Problem
The derived CPFL interpretation treated the explicit "Uso Sist Distr Ultr"
charge as a second ordinary demand row. The V3 comparison consequently tried
to split regular demand and excess into used/unused shares and stopped.
Separately, a sourced monthly cost with UNSPECIFIED tax metadata prevented
all closing totals even though its amount, effect and scenario were known.

Behavior
An exactly source-verified CPFL demand-excess label for the invoice month is
classified as DEMAND_EXCESS in the derived model. Its billed amount is retained
once; it is excluded from the ordinary demand quantity. OCR text, spans, native
confidence, the original invoice and the registered demand are unchanged.
Unknown labels and actual ambiguous used/unused partitions keep their checks.

The assisted closing preserves explicit nominal additional costs and credits
with UNSPECIFIED or EXCLUDED tax treatment without inventing tax values. Their
source, amount, nature, scenario and original treatment are recorded under
unit.sources.costReservations and described in the report. The comparison is
ESTIMATED and requires the existing human decision. This does not validate the
monthly cost record, infer exemption, gross up an unknown tax, approve or publish.
Supplier invoice/extra-energy deduplication and reconciliation remain mandatory.
Missing amounts, sources, nature or scenario still cannot produce a false total.

Waste presentation
The backoffice closing explicitly displays reactive excess and demand excess as
waste indicators using the same classification already used by the client portal.
They remain part of the original invoice and are never added to costs again.
The portal continues to consume only the approved published projection, scoped
to its authorized customer/unit, preserving the existing recommendations.

Scope
Existing backend and closing view, feature flags and interfaces. No database migration,
permission grant, new provider call or automatic financial decision. Immutable
previous versions remain intact. Preparing again creates the usual new version.

Validation
Regression coverage: source-bound label/month/field verification, OCR immutability,
preserved costs and credits, explicit tax reservations, no fractional cents,
no ungrounded amount, supplier reconciliation, V2/V3 taxes, identity and scope.
Private original-source reproduction is kept outside the repository.
